Research agenda

Reliable AI systems for embodied intelligence.

I am Akhilesh Veerapareddy, a software engineer developing a research direction at the intersection of AI systems, robot learning, and intelligent autonomous systems. I am interested in how timing, execution state, and evaluation shape the behavior of agents that plan and act.

Current stage: research preparation and study design. The proposed study below has no reported experimental results or peer-reviewed publication.

Questions I want to study

Time as part of the agent contract

How should a planning system account for inference delay, execution budgets, and deadlines before it commits to an action?

Reconstructable decisions

What must a system record so another researcher can replay a failure, inspect the decision boundary, and reproduce an evaluation?

From simulation to learned behavior

How can controlled simulation establish meaningful baselines before adding learned perception, language planners, or reinforcement learning policies?

First proposed study

Deadline-aware evaluation of simulated navigation pipelines

The proposed experiment compares waiting for a plan, checking a deadline before execution, and reserving an execution budget. Controlled planning delays and paired scenario seeds would make the tradeoff between timely completion, late completion, and explicit aborts measurable.

Environment
Static, single-robot simulation based on AutonomyOS
Primary outcome
Successful mission completion within a fixed wall-clock deadline
Status
Protocol drafted; implementation, pilot validation, and evaluation pending

This initial study would evaluate systems behavior in simulation. It would not establish physical robot safety, learned planning performance, or a novel planning algorithm.
